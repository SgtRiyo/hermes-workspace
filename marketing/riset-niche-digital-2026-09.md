# Riset Niche Produk Digital Etsy — September 2026

Untuk proyek [[../obsidian/proyek/etsy-digital-templates|Etsy digital templates]] (PRD editable templates).
Riset oleh Marketing sub-agent + Lead, 2026-09-21.

## Blocker sumber (transparansi)

- Etsy live search: **403 DataDome** (headless + curl, UA diganti) — tidak bisa scrape bestseller tag langsung.
- Google Trends: **429**, lalu timeout — tidak ada angka kurva.
- browser-harness: gagal attach Chrome headless.
- Yang jalan: Bing HTML + DuckDuckGo Lite → blog seller 2026 + halaman market Etsy.
- **Jangan klaim angka traffic — tidak ada data kuantitatif.**

## Evidence terkumpul (fakta, bukan tebakan)

- **Canva-compatible template = kategori #1** digital download (craftpilot.io, "Etsy Digital Downloads Trends 2026-2027"). Sub: social media, business branding, wedding suite, resume. Template yang jalan di **Canva tier gratis** paling laku.
- **Wall art bundle 4-6 karya** (gallery-wall cohesive set) kalahkan single print. Gaya: minimalist line art, botanical, abstract watercolor.
- **Digital planner**: hyperlinked, wajib kompatibel **GoodNotes/Notability**; fitur: habit tracker, meal planner, budget sheet, goal-setting; sediakan **dated + undated**; sticker pack add-on = repeat purchase.
- **SVG cut file**: demand stabil, pasar matang → menang di niche tema + **commercial license upsell**.
- Best-seller nyata designinstantly.com (harga $3): "14500+ Canva PLR Templates Bundle", "400+ Faceless Instagram Carousel Templates | TikTok & IG Slides", "7500 DFY PLR/MRR Bundle – planners, journals, trackers" → **PLR/MRR bundle + faceless carousel = pola paling laku sekarang**.
- Sumber pendukung (belum dibaca detail): ehunt.ai, creatifystore, resellready, litcommerce, merchtitans, sellertoolshq.

## Top niche (peringkat kasar)

1. **Faceless social media carousel bundle** (IG+TikTok, Canva, 4:5 & 9:16) — evergreen, demand terbukti, cocok pemula.
2. **Digital planner undated** (GoodNotes/Notability) + habit/meal/budget tracker — evergreen, upsell sticker pack.
3. **Small-business bundle** (invoice, price list, brand kit Canva) — harga lebih tinggi.
4. Wedding suite Canva — seasonal musim pernikahan.
5. Resume/CV template — evergreen, kompetisi tinggi.
6. Wall art bundle (nursery/office) — butuh asset grafis lebih banyak.
7. Planner sticker pack — bagus sebagai add-on, bukan produk utama.
8. Teacher/classroom printable — evergreen, niche kompetisi sedang.

## Rekomendasi 3 prioritas (produk pertama)

1. **Faceless social media carousel/bundle** — modular, tanpa skill desain, target content creator.
2. **Undated hyperlinked digital planner + tracker** — evergreen, add-on sticker = repeat purchase.
3. **Small-business bundle** (invoice/price list/brand kit) — ticket size lebih tinggi.

## Tindak lanjut

- Pilih 1 niche untuk produk pertama → lanjut Fase 2 PRD (master template).
- Validasi manual di Etsy app (pencarian kata kunci, cek "bestseller badge") — tidak bisa diotomasi karena DataDome.
