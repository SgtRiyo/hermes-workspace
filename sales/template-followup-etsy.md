# Etsy Client Follow-up Template

## Subject Line Options:
- Order update: [Order ID] - [Customer Name]
- Question about: [Product/Item Name]
- Shipping status: [Order ID] - [Customer Name]
- {order_update/question/update}_shipping}_status}_{order_id}_{customer_name}

## Template Structure:

**Subject:**
[Clear, specific reference to order/product + brief action request]

**Greeting:**
Hi [Customer Name],

**Opening:**
Acknowledge their Etsy purchase/order (#), reference specific item, express appreciation

**Body:**
- Order/Transaction Reference: [Order #]
- Item Details: [Product Name], [Quantity], [Key Features]
- Current Status: [Clear status update]
- Next Steps/Action Needed: [Specific, time-bound request]

**Closing:**
Appreciation statement + Offer for additional assistance + Professional signature

**Example Follow-up #1 (Order Update):**
Subject: Your Order #[ORDER-123] - Item Ready for Shipping!

Hi [Customer Name],

Thank you for your purchase of [Product Name] from your Etsy store! I'm excited to let you know your order is now packaged and ready for shipment. We expect it to ship within 24-48 hours.

Order Details:
- Order #[ORDER-123]
- Product: [Product Name]
- Quantity: [Quantity]
- Status: Processing → Ready for Shipping

Next Steps:
- Your tracking number will be available on your Etsy purchases page within 24 hours of shipment
- Expect delivery in [X-X] business days

Thanks for choosing your Etsy store! Let me know if you need any updates before then.

Best regards,
fern

**Example Follow-up #2 (Question/Clarification):**
Subject: Quick question about [Product Name] from your Etsy order

Hi [Customer Name],

I noticed you inquired about [Product Name] from order #[ORDER-456]. Let me provide the details you requested:

Product Info:
- Material: [Material details]
- Dimensions: [Size/Measurements]
- Care Instructions: [Cleaning notes]

Does this cover what you needed? Let me know if you have any other questions!

Thanks,
fern

**Template Variables to Customize:**
- [Customer Name] -> Use full name for personalization
- [Product Name] -> Include specific product identifier
- [Order ID] -> Clear order reference number
- [Product Specifications] -> Key details about the item
- [Action Item] -> Specific request from customer

**Communication Guidelines:**
1. Keep responses under 200 words for readability
2. Use clear subject lines with order references
3. Include specific product details for easy reference
4. End with specific next steps when possible
5. Follow up on customer responses within 24 hours
6. Maintain consistent tone: friendly but professional
